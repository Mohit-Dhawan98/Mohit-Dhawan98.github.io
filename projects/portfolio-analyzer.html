PortfolioAnalyzer

Keeps the facts about your investments with a source and a date on each, so an assistant can cite them instead of guessing.

Kind
Art and tools
Where
Open source
Links
Repository

The problem

Ask a language model about a portfolio and it answers from memory: a ratio with no date and a different figure each time.

What was built

A local pipeline for Indian equities and mutual funds that fetches fundamentals and fund prices, stores each with its source and as-of date, scores them with a fixed rubric and writes a digest and a spreadsheet. It is tooling, not investment advice.

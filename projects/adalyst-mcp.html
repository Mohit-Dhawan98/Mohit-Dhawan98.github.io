adalyst-mcp

Lets an AI assistant look up a brand's live ads on Facebook and Instagram and describe what they are doing.

Kind

The problem

Checking what competitors are advertising means searching ad libraries by hand, one brand and one ad at a time.

What was built

An MCP server that searches a brand's current Meta ads and analyzes the creatives, the messaging and the opening of each video, for one brand or several side by side.

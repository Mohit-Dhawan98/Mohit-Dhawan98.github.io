Steps taken, and the error where the run stands now. The small curve is error against steps.
The ground is a model’s error for every setting it could take: high ground is high error, valleys are low. It is cut into numbered regions by height and painted the way photo-to-paint paints a photo. Press paint to fill them in.
The orange path is gradient descent: each step goes downhill, and the steps shorten as the ground flattens. Click the ground to start a run there.
